# Clearway — Submission Blurb

I built Clearway, an AI-powered first-touch intake for property insurance
claims. A claimant describes what happened in ordinary language; Clearway
turns that account into visible facts, identifies what is still missing, and
asks the next question that matters for handling the case. It then produces a
non-binding property-adjuster recommendation or sends uncertain and urgent
cases to human review rather than guessing.

I chose this problem because water damage has caused real stress in my own
life, both growing up and now while living in an apartment with shared walls
and plumbing. Traditional digital intake relies on fixed questionnaires, even
though the useful follow-up depends on what the claimant just said. Clearway
uses AI to make that conversation adaptive, so a claim can reach a person with
more specific, structured context instead of another generic form response.
