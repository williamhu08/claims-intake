# Clearway — Submission Blurb

Water damage has caused real stress in my life. Growing up, water getting into
our kitchen floor led to arguments about the cause, responsibility, and what to
do next. Now that I live in an apartment with shared walls and plumbing, those
questions feel even more immediate. Filing an insurance claim can start another
long loop: an insurer reviews the account, asks a follow-up based on it, waits
for a response, and repeats. I built Clearway to shorten that loop. A claimant
describes what happened in ordinary language; Clearway extracts the known
facts, shows what is missing, and asks the next relevant question. The result is
a more specific, structured case before a person needs to step in.

Clearway's AI is adaptive but bounded. After each response, the model may ask
one targeted question, propose a non-binding route, or escalate to a person.
The application—not the model—owns the signed session, validates every update,
tracks where facts came from, and decides when the process must stop. Unsafe or
unresolved cases go to human review rather than being guessed through. For
completed water-damage intakes, fixed rules produce the final next step:
property-adjuster review, human review, or urgent human review. The model never
invents policy logic, and Clearway does not decide coverage, fault, valuation,
payment, or settlement. I kept the scope narrow to demonstrate one defensible
claims workflow from first account to actionable handoff.
